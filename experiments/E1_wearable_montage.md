# E1 — Wearable Montage Experiment

## 1. Research Question
Does replacing the historical 8-channel EEG montage with the intended wearable 8-channel montage change strict LOSO performance when everything else remains unchanged?

## 2. Hypothesis
The wearable montage (frontal + temporal + parietal layout) captures sufficient spatial auditory attention features such that performance will either be maintained or slightly improved compared to the historical montage, which was centered heavily on the motor cortex.

## 3. E0 Configuration (Reference)
- **Model:** Contrastive MatchNet (`eegnet` encoder)
- **Audio:** 28-band Gammatone Envelopes (Historical FIR)
- **EEG Montage:** `[13, 46, 43, 23, 50, 0, 52, 14]` (C5, FCz, FC6, P9, C6, Fp1, TP8, T7)
- **Window Length:** 10.0 seconds (benchmark reference)
- **Expected Accuracy:** ~69.02% (Historical baseline)

## 4. E1 Configuration
- **EEG Montage:** `[0, 33, 6, 41, 22, 59, 15, 52]` (Fp1, Fp2, F7, F8, P7, P8, TP7, TP8)

## 5. Controlled Variables
Every other aspect of the pipeline was kept identical to E0, guaranteeing that the $\Delta$ is entirely attributable to the spatial topography change.

- **Branch:** `experiment/e1-wearable-montage`

## 9. Interpretation: Why did performance collapse?
The -9.82% collapse completely falsifies the hypothesis. The wearable montage is severely inferior to the historical montage for Auditory Attention Decoding (AAD). 

**The Neurophysiological Reason:**
1. **Loss of Fronto-Central Dipoles:** The strongest Auditory Evoked Potentials (e.g., N100, P200) generated by the superior temporal gyrus project strongly to the top of the head (Cz / FCz). The historical `E0` montage contained `FCz`, `FC6`, `C5`, and `C6`, directly capturing this high-SNR auditory response.
2. **The Wearable "Blindspot":** The `E1` montage (`Fp1, Fp2, F7, F8, P7, P8, TP7, TP8`) entirely amputates the Fronto-Central/Central electrodes. It forms a ring around the periphery of the head (forehead + behind ears). 
3. **Artifact Contamination:** `Fp1`, `Fp2`, `F7`, and `F8` are notoriously contaminated by ocular (eye blink) and muscular (jaw) artifacts, effectively contributing noise rather than signal.

**Conclusion:** MatchNet is heavily reliant on the high-SNR fronto-central auditory dipoles. Confining the electrodes to a strictly peripheral wearable form-factor deprives the spatial filter of its most important spatial features.

## 10. Limitations
The evaluation proves that *linear/shallow* spatial filtering (as used in the EEGNet encoder) cannot recover the auditory envelope from the peripheral wearable montage. It remains to be seen if advanced non-linear spatial representations (e.g., Self-Attention) can extract sufficient signal from the remaining temporal electrodes (TP7/TP8/P7/P8).
